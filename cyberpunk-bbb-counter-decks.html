BBB Counter-Netrun

Five mono-Blue (3 Blue Legends) decks for Cyberpunk TCG – Welcome to Night City, built to beat BBG (Alt / Hanako / Jackie), Johnny Silverhand: Never Stop Fighting and Adam Smasher: Ender of Legends. Each is 40 cards plus an optional 7-card sideboard, max 3 copies per card counting the sideboard.

Know your enemy

BBG – Alt / Hanako / Jackie

Program control, the #2 deck in the meta. Usually 26–28 Programs and 10–14 Units.
  • Pyramid Song gives -4 power, and with a min d4 it also bottom-decks power-0 Units.
  • Towerfall gives -5 to all of your Units, and can also bottom-deck everything at power 0.
  • Les Élémens bottom-decks your lowest-power Unit.
  • Lizzy Wizzy, Maman Brigitte and Placide are their Unit threats.

Plan: avoid power-0 Units, keep a cheap decoy Unit out to soak up Les Élémens, and play a few bodies with 6+ power. Their removal runs at sorcery speed.

Johnny Silverhand – Never Stop Fighting

Red Unit, cost 6, power 8. "The first time this Unit wins a fight each turn, ready it." "This Unit wins all fights against CORPO Units."
  • Never block him with a CORPO Unit. Leave out Hacked Corpo, and don't take V: Corporate Exile as a Legend.
  • He only readies after winning a fight. If he steals unblocked, he stays spent, which turns on Unlikely Bond.
  • Smasher lists put Mantis Blades on him. Use Maman Brigitte (unequipped Units only) before the Gear lands.

Adam Smasher – Ender of Legends

Red Legend. Go Solo for 9, power 9, can attack the turn he arrives, Play: defeat a rival Unit. If he leaves the field, he is removed from the game.
  • Any bottom-deck effect deletes him for the whole game: Unlikely Bond (after he attacks), Placide's attack, Maman Brigitte (if unequipped).
  • Chrome Reverie / Nocturne ("can't attack") wastes his 9 Eddies.
  • His Play defeats one Unit. Don't put all your power into a single body; keep 2–3 mid-size Units out.
  • He costs 9 Eddies, so make them spend it while you're already ahead on Gigs.

Before you sleeve up

  • RAM: three Blue Legends give 6 Blue RAM, so every Blue card is legal. The highest Blue RAM is Judy Álvarez: Nothing to Doubt at 5.
  • Sell Tags: I couldn't confirm which cards carry a Sell Tag. Count yours and aim for at least 30% (12 or more of 40) so you hit your Eddies. If you're short, swap a 1-of for a Sell Tag card.
  • Hanako's text wasn't checked. Read it before your BBG games and adjust your Gig plan.
  • Card text comes from ripperdeck.gg and the meta data from ripperdeck/exburst (Nov 2026). Double-check anything surprising against the official database at cyberpunktcg.com.
