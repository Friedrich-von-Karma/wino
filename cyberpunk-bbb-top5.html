Know your enemy

BBG – Alt / Hanako / Jackie

Program control, the #2 deck in the meta. Usually 26–28 Programs and 10–14 Units.
  • Pyramid Song gives -4 power, and with a min d4 it also bottom-decks power-0 Units.
  • Towerfall gives -5 to all of your Units, and can also bottom-deck everything at power 0.
  • Les Élémens bottom-decks your lowest-power Unit.
  • Lizzy Wizzy, Maman Brigitte and Placide are their Unit threats.

General plan: avoid power-0 Units, keep a cheap decoy Unit out to soak up Les Élémens, play 6+ power bodies, and don't commit more than 3 Units at once into a possible Towerfall.

Johnny Silverhand – Never Stop Fighting

Red Unit, cost 6, power 8. "The first time this Unit wins a fight each turn, ready it." "This Unit wins all fights against CORPO Units."
  • Never block him with a CORPO Unit (Hacked Corpo, a V: Corporate Exile that has gone solo).
  • He only readies after winning a fight. Unblocked, he stays spent, which turns on Unlikely Bond.
  • Answer him with Maman Brigitte before Mantis Blades (or other Gear) lands.
  • Make him lose a fight: block, then Pyramid Song (-4) / Rogue (-4) / Floor It (-1) / Wakako (-2).

Adam Smasher – Ender of Legends

Red Legend. Go Solo for 9, power 9, can attack the turn he arrives, Play: defeat a rival Unit. If he leaves the field, he is removed from the game.
  • Any bottom-deck deletes him for the rest of the game: Unlikely Bond (after he attacks), Placide's attack, Maman Brigitte (if unequipped), Les Élémens (if he's their smallest Unit).
  • Chrome Reverie / Nocturne ("can't attack") wastes his 9 Eddies.
  • Keep a cheap Unit out to absorb his Play defeat.
  • He costs 9 Eddies, so make them spend it while you're already ahead on Gigs.

Before you sleeve up

  • RAM: three Blue Legends give 6 Blue RAM, so every Blue card is legal. The highest Blue RAM is Judy Álvarez: Nothing to Doubt at 5.
  • Sell Tags: I couldn't confirm which cards carry a Sell Tag. Count yours and aim for at least 30% (12 or more of 40) so you hit your Eddies every turn. If you're short, swap a 1-of for a Sell Tag card.
  • Turn numbers in the plans assume you sell one card a turn and hit your Eddies. Adjust if you're behind.
  • Hanako's text wasn't checked. Read it before your BBG games.
